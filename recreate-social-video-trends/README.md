# Recreate Social Video Trends

## What it does

Researches short-form video patterns, breaks down reference editing techniques, and rebuilds their visual language as original, editable Remotion templates.

## Why use it

Use it to adapt a trend without producing a hard-coded copy, leaving clips, timing, text, branding, and exports reusable for later videos. See [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) for its operating instructions.
