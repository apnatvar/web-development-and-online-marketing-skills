# Opinion And Disagreement

Disagreement should be specific and grounded in operational constraints.

Prefer "this claim misses the maintenance problem" over broad attacks on the people making the claim.

When criticizing a trend, name the useful part first if it exists, then explain the hidden cost or missing condition.
