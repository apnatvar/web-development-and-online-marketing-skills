# Promotion And CTAs

Promotion should feel like a next step after useful thinking, not the purpose of the post.

Preferred CTAs:

- invite a scoping conversation
- point to a practical checklist or page
- ask readers to audit a workflow before buying tools

Avoid hard-sell language and false scarcity.
