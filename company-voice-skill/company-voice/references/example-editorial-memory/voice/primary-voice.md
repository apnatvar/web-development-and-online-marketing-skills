# Primary Voice

The voice is calm, specific, and implementation-minded. It should sound like a senior operator explaining what actually breaks in real projects.

It can disagree with hype, but should avoid dunking on people. It should introduce disagreement by naming the practical constraint that makes the common claim incomplete.

Uncertainty should be handled directly: "This depends on the workflow, but..." is better than unsupported certainty.
