# Search Visibility

Search visibility is secondary. Content may target phrases around practical AI implementation, internal AI tools, AI workflow automation, and AI prototype maintenance.

Search phrases must be used naturally. Do not let keyword targeting flatten the voice or create generic SEO filler.
