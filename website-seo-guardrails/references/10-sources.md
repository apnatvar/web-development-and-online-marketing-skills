## Source discipline

Prefer official search-engine policies for compliance and eligibility. Use SEO-platform documentation for its tool operation and as practitioner guidance, not as proof of a search engine's undisclosed ranking behavior. Record access date, direct URL, quoted fact, and whether a conclusion is observed or inferred.
