# Practical training curriculum

Use this only when the user asks to train a team or interns. Give each learner one specialization with an audit and implementation exercise. Consolidate findings in a shared weekly review and require evidence, acceptance tests, and limitations.

| Track | Topic | Practical assignment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Topic clusters and intent | Map one business topic into a pillar, distinct spokes, and bottom-of-funnel pages. |
| 2 | Internal linking | Find orphan pages and propose contextual links with destination rationale. |
| 3 | Technical indexing | Audit robots, canonicals, sitemaps, status codes, rendering, and duplicates. |
| 4 | Content refresh | Find decaying pages and write evidence-based refresh briefs. |
| 5 | Original research | Design one publishable benchmark or study with methodology and limitations. |
| 6 | Digital PR | Build a qualified target list and outreach angles for the research asset. |
| 7 | Entity and schema | Audit Organization, Person/ProfilePage, and site-relevant structured data. |
| 8 | AI visibility | Build a representative prompt set across intent families and define separate citation/mention metrics. |
| 9 | Ecommerce | Audit categories, facets, product data, variants, and feed readiness. |
| 10 | Local SEO | Audit business profiles, core details, reviews, and location pages. |
| 11 | Multimedia | Audit image and video discoverability, accessibility, and structured data. |
| 12 | International | Review locale URL architecture, localization depth, and hreflang. |

## Review rubric

Score each assignment on:

1. Evidence quality and source hierarchy.
2. Correct diagnosis of the visibility layer.
3. User and business value.
4. Technical feasibility and risk.
5. Clear acceptance tests.
6. Measurement that separates visibility, citation, mention, and conversion.
7. Recognition of unknowns and time-sensitive guidance.
