## Verification notes

At the 20 September 2026 validation pass, Google documentation supported these time-sensitive claims:

- Core SEO practices remain relevant to Google generative-search features; there is no special required AI schema.
- Google Search does not use `llms.txt` as a ranking or visibility signal.
- Dedicated generative-AI visibility reports exist in Search Console and expose page, country, device, and date views as documented.
- Google removed documentation for the FAQ rich-result feature after the feature stopped appearing in Search in 2026.

OpenAI's current publisher documentation says public sites can appear in ChatGPT search and advises allowing `OAI-SearchBot` for content discovery, summaries, and clear citations. Keep OAI-SearchBot search controls distinct from GPTBot training controls.
