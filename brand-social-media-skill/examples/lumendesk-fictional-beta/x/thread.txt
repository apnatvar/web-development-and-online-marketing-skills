# X thread — decision records

## Post 1

A final decision is not the same as a useful decision trail. The source may be in a note, the uncertainty in chat, and the conclusion in a roadmap.

## Post 2

For decisions worth revisiting, keep four fields together: source, open question, conclusion, and owner.

## Post 3

The tradeoff is maintenance. Use the record selectively, define ownership, and archive it when it no longer matters.

## Post 4
