# Editorial campaign summary

This is an editorial quality review of fictional drafts, not evidence of performance.

## Outcome

The campaign uses one verified fictional mechanism—the four-field decision record—while giving each platform a distinct role. Community drafts reduce CTA strength, outreach sequences require source-backed personalization and decline paths, long form teaches the method independently, and visual briefs avoid fabricated UI.
