# Publishing sequence

1. Publish the fictional field guide as the primary long-form source.
2. Share the method on LinkedIn and the newsletter with distinct professional and subscriber framing.
3. Publish the Instagram carousel and X observation to visualise and compress the method.
4. Open the conversational Threads and Facebook versions.
5. Research a live subreddit and participate before considering the drafted Reddit contribution; this example does not identify a real subreddit.
6. Use DMs and cold email only after the sender confirms targeting, relationship, jurisdiction, approved footer, and opt-out operations.

Repurpose facts and the four-field record, not sentences. Suggested cadence is three fictional posts per week over four weeks; actual timing requires user approval and account context.
