# Messaging pillars

## P1 — Preserve the why

The useful unit is not just a final decision; it is the source, open question, conclusion, and owner that explain the decision.

## P2 — Use a small independent method

Teams can test the four-field decision record in their existing tools. This gives the campaign standalone educational value.

## P3 — Organise around decisions

Fictional LumenDesk groups research artifacts by decision rather than by document folder. This is stated positioning, not independent proof of superiority.

## P4 — Invite scrutiny, not hype

The product is a fictional beta with no customer results. The campaign invites appropriate teams to inspect the method and product rather than promising an outcome.
