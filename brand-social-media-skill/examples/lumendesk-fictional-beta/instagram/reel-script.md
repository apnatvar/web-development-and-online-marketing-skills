# Reel script: the disappearing decision trail

## Opening visual and spoken hook

Visual: a source card, chat bubble, and roadmap card slide away from each other.

Voiceover: “A decision can survive while its reasoning disappears.”

## Shot sequence

1. Show the separated source, question, and conclusion. On-screen text: “Scattered.”
2. Move them into one card. Voiceover: “Keep the source, open question, conclusion, and owner together.”
3. Show the four-field record in a neutral document. Voiceover: “Test the method in tools you already use.”
4. Show a labelled fictional LumenDesk mockup. Voiceover: “It also shaped our fictional beta.”
5. End card: “Join the fictional beta.”

## Caption and accessibility

Use the copy in `caption.txt`. Burn accurate captions into the video and provide a transcript. Describe important visual changes in the voiceover. Do not show fabricated customer data or unlabelled product functionality.
