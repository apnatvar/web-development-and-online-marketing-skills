# Instagram carousel brief

- Platform: Instagram.
- Purpose: teach a four-field decision record.
- Associated content: `instagram/carousel.md`.
- Aspect ratio: verify the current recommended portrait ratio in the composer; use one consistent ratio.
- Subject: four labelled cards—source, open question, conclusion, owner—joining one decision record.
- Composition: one field per educational slide, then a combined final record.
- Visual hierarchy: cover question, field name, one-sentence explanation.
- Text overlay: fewer than 22 words per slide.
- Brand: fictional cream and indigo system, restrained line icons.
- Accessibility: custom alt text per slide; high contrast; do not communicate state by colour alone.
- Negative constraints: no customer logos, metrics, testimonials, real messages, or unlabelled UI.
- Prompt: Minimal editorial carousel for a fictional research workflow, cream background, indigo cards, clear hierarchy, generous whitespace, four fields flowing into one decision record, no logos, no tiny text.
- Filename: `instagram-decision-record-carousel-01.png` through `-05.png`.
