A research decision rarely disappears all at once.

The source link moves to one document. The open question stays in chat. The conclusion enters a roadmap. Six months later, the decision remains—but its reasoning is hard to reconstruct.

Our fictional team now uses a four-field record:

1. Source
2. Open question
3. Conclusion
4. Owner

We built the fictional LumenDesk beta around that same unit of work: the decision, not the folder where a document happens to live.

There are no customer results behind this example. The useful part is the method, which a small team can test in tools it already uses.
