# Cold-email personalisation and risk notes

- Source for the example: a clearly fictional public conference note by “Morgan.”
- Replace `{{sender_postal_address}}`, `{{approved_sender_identification}}`, and `{{opt_out_method}}` only with text approved for the actual sender and jurisdiction.
- Verify the recipient's role, relationship, jurisdiction, lawful basis or consent requirements, source provenance, suppression status, and sending-provider rules.
- Stop after a decline or opt-out. Do not infer that silence is interest.
- Do not use fabricated reply prefixes, forwarding markers, customer proof, or performance claims.
