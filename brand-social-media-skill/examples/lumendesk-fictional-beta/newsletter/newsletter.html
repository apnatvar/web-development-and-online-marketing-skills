A final decision can remain visible long after its reasoning has scattered across notes, chat, and roadmaps.

Try four fields

For a decision your team may need to revisit, keep the source, open question, conclusion, and owner together. The method works in a document or wiki; the important choice is which decisions deserve the maintenance.

The tradeoff

Recording everything creates noise. We suggest starting with a decision that is costly to reverse, depends on uncertain evidence, or will affect several functions. Then decide who should update or archive the record.
