# Audience

- Primary audience: Research leads at small product teams
- Secondary audience: Product managers who coordinate research
- Buyer versus user: both
- Industry: Software
- Organisation size: 2–20 person teams
- Technical sophistication: moderate
- Existing alternatives: Documents, wikis, spreadsheets
- Pain points: Lost source context, unclear decision history
- Objections: Another workspace may add process
- Desired outcome: Understand why a research-backed decision was made
- Awareness level: problem_aware
- Geographic focus: Fictional global market
