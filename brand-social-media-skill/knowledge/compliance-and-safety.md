# Compliance and safety

## Publication gate

Before writing public content, scan imported context for credentials, private tokens, personal data, private emails, internal-only URLs, private repository links, confidential customer names, and unpublished financial data. Stop and flag suspected sensitive material.

## Outreach

Do not claim universal legal compliance. Outreach law differs by jurisdiction, recipient, relationship, and message purpose. Require the user to verify consent or another lawful basis, sender identification, recordkeeping, address requirements, and opt-out handling before sending. Keep visible legal-footer and opt-out placeholders until the sender supplies approved text.

Avoid deception, fake familiarity, fabricated personalization, hidden affiliation, scraping instructions, indiscriminate bulk contact, and attempts to evade platform controls. Give recipients an easy decline path.

## Communities

Read current community rules before drafting a promotional group or subreddit post. Do not recommend vote manipulation, coordinated engagement, ban evasion, fake accounts, or mass-posted variants. Disclose affiliation clearly.

## Review boundary

The validators detect structural risks and suspicious patterns; they are not legal, privacy, security, or platform approval. Escalate high-risk industries, regulated claims, contests, political content, employment decisions, health claims, and financial claims for qualified review.
