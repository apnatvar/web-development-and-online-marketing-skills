# Content repurposing

## Content map

Map each output to a message pillar, audience state, channel role, content format, proof item, CTA, and source link in `strategy/content-map.md`.

## Transform, do not transpose

- LinkedIn: operational or professional significance.
- Facebook: accessible explanation or community discussion.
- Reddit: problem-first contribution tailored to one community.
- Instagram: visual sequence and concise teaching.
- X: compressed observation or logically connected thread.
- Threads: conversational thought and response invitation.
- Medium: standalone depth and useful structure.
- DM: permission-aware, personalized conversation.
- Cold email: specific relevance and a small commercial ask.
- Newsletter: relationship-based value and product context.

Do not create one master paragraph and resize it. A shared fact may appear across channels, but the opening, evidence order, framing, and action should change.

## Duplication review

Use `scripts/validate_outputs.py` to flag near-identical cross-platform copy. Review manually because similar legal language, product names, and disclosures are legitimate.
