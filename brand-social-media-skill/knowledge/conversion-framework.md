# Conversion framework

## Match intent to audience state

- Unaware: surface a recognizable problem; use a low-friction learning CTA.
- Problem-aware: clarify the cost, cause, or failed workaround.
- Solution-aware: explain the mechanism and tradeoffs.
- Product-aware: address fit, proof, implementation, price, or risk.
- Most aware: make the offer and next step explicit.

## CTA ladder

Choose the least forceful CTA that can meet the channel's role:

1. Reflect or respond.
2. Save or read a guide.
3. View documentation or a demonstration.
4. Join a waitlist or beta.
5. Reply, book, start a trial, or buy.

Use one primary CTA per asset. A secondary CTA must not compete with it. Do not manufacture urgency or scarcity.

## Measurement suggestions

Suggest measures that correspond to the objective: qualified replies, documentation visits, demo requests, trial starts, subscriber retention, or community participation. Separate platform metrics from business outcomes. Never promise a metric or present an editorial score as performance evidence.
