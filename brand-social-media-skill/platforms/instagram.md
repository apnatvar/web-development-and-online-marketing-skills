# Instagram

- Last verified: 2026-07-22
- Official constraint: Instagram supports custom alt text for photo posts.
- Source: [edit alternative text](https://www.facebook.com/help/instagram/503708446705527/)
- Unknown numeric constraints: verify caption and hashtag behavior in the current composer; do not treat null registry values as unlimited.

## Purpose and audience behavior

Use Instagram for visual product communication, concise education, launch storytelling, founder context, carousels, Reels, and Stories. The visual and opening line must work together; captions should deepen rather than transcribe the asset.

## Formats

Support standard image and carousel captions, carousel slide copy, Reels captions and scripts, Story sequences, product launches, education, founder posts, CTA variants, grouped hashtags, image descriptions, alt text, and visual briefs.

## Hook and structure

- Image post: visual premise, opening caption line, compact explanation, CTA.
- Carousel: cover promise, narrative sequence, one main idea per slide, final CTA slide, caption, visual direction, alt text per informative image.
- Reel: opening visual, spoken hook, shot sequence, voiceover, on-screen text, final CTA, caption, and visual notes.
- Story: one beat per frame, legible overlay, interaction only when useful, and a clear final action.

## Hashtags, links, and visuals

Keep hashtags separate unless the user asks otherwise. Use a small relevant set grouped as branded, niche, audience, topic, location, or campaign; omit irrelevant categories. Do not promise reach. State where the usable link will live and do not invent a link sticker or account feature.

Write alt text for every informative still. Caption spoken content and include key information in the accompanying text. Use real UI or an explicitly labelled mockup.

## Failure modes

Reject generic high-volume hashtag padding, caption-only carousels, slides overloaded with copy, fabricated product UI, tiny text, unsupported before-and-after claims, and direct copies of LinkedIn text.

## Validation checklist

- Confirm format and visual mode.
- Check one idea per carousel slide or one beat per shot.
- Separate and relevance-check hashtags.
- Add alt text, image descriptions, and video captions/transcript.
- Verify current composer limits and CTA feature availability.

## Fictional example

**Cover:** Where did the decision reasoning go?

**Slides:** 1) The source moved. 2) The question stayed in chat. 3) The conclusion entered a roadmap. 4) Fictional LumenDesk groups all three around one decision. 5) Join the fictional beta.

**Alt text:** Five simple cream-and-indigo slides showing a scattered research trail becoming one organised decision record. No customer data appears.
