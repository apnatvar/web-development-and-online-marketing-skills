# Medium or long-form blog

- Last verified: 2026-07-22
- Official constraints: Medium disallows duplicate Medium stories; owned work published elsewhere may be cross-posted with a canonical link. Traffic-only clipped stories can violate spam rules.
- Sources: [canonical links](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/360033930293-Set-a-canonical-link), [duplicate content](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/360039513913-About-the-No-Duplicate-Content-rule), [Medium rules](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/213477928-Medium-Rules)
- Volatility rule: recheck rules before syndication or strong offsite CTAs.

## Purpose and audience behavior

Use long form for standalone explanation, authority, tutorials, comparisons, case studies, launch context, founder essays, technical education, and use-case detail. The article must repay the reader without requiring a product click.

## Formats and package

Support detailed articles, shorter product essays, technical explainers, use cases, comparisons, founder essays, tutorials, case studies, and launch articles. Generate title options, subtitle, slug, excerpt, article, heading structure, internal links, external-source placeholders, CTA, topics, canonical recommendation, metadata, image brief, and alt text.

## Structure

1. Promise a useful question or outcome in the title and opening.
2. Establish the problem and audience context.
3. Explain the mechanism, evidence, tradeoffs, or procedure.
4. Use examples and source-supported claims.
5. Connect the product only where it genuinely advances the explanation.
6. Close with a useful summary and proportionate CTA.

## Publication and links

Record whether this is the primary publication, owned-site cross-post, syndication, or differentiated version. Set a canonical link to the original owned source when applicable. Do not create duplicate copies on Medium. Disclose offsite destinations when current Medium rules require it. Use descriptive link text.

## Accessibility and visuals

Use descriptive headings, useful alt text, readable tables, transcripts for media, and real or labelled product visuals. Avoid keyword padding and images containing essential text only.

## Failure modes

Reject padded advertisements, fabricated citations, false comparisons, clipped teasers whose only purpose is traffic, undisclosed affiliate or offsite forms, mechanical duplication, and unowned content reuse.

## Validation checklist

- Confirm ownership, publication type, and canonical source.
- Verify every external claim and link placeholder.
- Ensure standalone value and logical headings.
- Add metadata, image brief, and alt text.
- Check current Medium rules before publishing.
