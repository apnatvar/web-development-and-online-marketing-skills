# LinkedIn

- Last verified: 2026-07-22
- Official constraints: post 3,000 characters; article 125,000 characters.
- Sources: [posting updates](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a528176/), [posts versus articles](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a522483/differences-between-posting-updates-and-publishing)
- Volatility rule: recheck `config/platform-constraints.json` and the account composer before publication.

## Purpose and audience behavior

Use LinkedIn for professional authority, operational relevance, founder perspective, B2B discovery, product education, case evidence, and direct promotion. Readers scan for a concrete reason to continue and expect the author to have a credible relationship to the subject.

## Formats

Support short, standard, and long posts; articles; company-page and founder-led posts; authority, educational, product, case-study, build-in-public, contrarian-analysis, and promotional categories; document or carousel outlines; first-comment link plans; alternative hooks; CTA alternatives; and visual accessibility text.

## Hook and body

- Lead with a specific observation, operational tension, result with evidence, tradeoff, or counterintuitive claim.
- Establish the audience and consequence early.
- Develop one idea with concrete mechanisms, examples, or evidence.
- Close with a proportionate action or useful question.

Choose the requested category intentionally. Label a personal account opinion as the founder's view; do not invent a personal story for emotional effect.

## CTA, links, and visuals

Use direct CTAs for product-aware readers and lighter resource or discussion CTAs for educational posts. Put the link in the post unless the user specifically requests a first-comment plan; do not claim that one placement receives more reach. Give document posts one idea per page, a clear cover, a coherent sequence, and an accessible text equivalent.

## Formatting and accessibility

Use readable paragraphs, purposeful line breaks, and descriptive links. Avoid walls of one-sentence fragments. Provide alt text or an image description for meaningful visuals. Caption video and do not place the full argument inside an image.

## Failure modes

Reject fake personal stories, empty motivation, unsupported statistics, exaggerated claims, generic “agree?” endings, engagement bait, and jargon-only copy. Do not write a company-page post as if it were a founder's personal experience.

## Validation checklist

- Confirm category, author identity, audience, claim sources, CTA, and link placement.
- Check the current character limit.
- Preserve product naming and qualification.
- Verify that the hook is specific and the body supplies value.
- Add accessibility text for visuals.
