# Threads

- Last verified: 2026-07-22
- Official constraints: post 500 characters; optional text attachment up to 10,000 characters where available.
- Sources: [Threads introduction](https://about.fb.com/news/2023/07/introducing-threads-new-app-text-sharing/), [text attachments](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/09/attach-text-threads-posts-share-longer-perspectives/)
- Volatility rule: confirm text-attachment availability and composer behavior on the publishing account.

## Purpose and audience behavior

Use Threads for conversational product observations, founder commentary, informal teaching, discussion prompts, launches, and thought sequences. Readers expect a human conversational rhythm; preserve brand voice without copying X compression or Instagram hashtags.

## Formats

Support concise standalone posts, conversational threads, product observations, founder commentary, informal educational sequences, discussion prompts, launch notes, and optional long-text attachments.

## Hook and body

- Start with an observation someone might naturally say aloud.
- Add enough context to invite a useful response.
- Let a sequence unfold through related thoughts, not numbered article chunks.
- Use a text attachment for genuinely longer context while keeping the main post meaningful by itself.

## CTA, links, and visuals

Use conversational CTAs: ask for experience, offer a useful resource, or invite a proportionate next step. Do not carry over Instagram hashtag blocks. Give links a clear reason to click. Provide alt text and captions for visual or spoken content.

## Failure modes

Reject X copy with extra words, fake casualness, unexplained links, generic engagement prompts, hashtag blocks, unsupported reach claims, and a text attachment that exists only to hide a long advertisement.

## Validation checklist

- Keep the main post within the verified standard limit.
- Confirm account access before relying on text attachments.
- Preserve conversational cadence and one clear premise.
- Check source claims, CTA, links, and accessibility.
- Differentiate the result from the X and Instagram versions.
