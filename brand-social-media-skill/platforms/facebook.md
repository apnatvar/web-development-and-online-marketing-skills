# Facebook

- Last verified: 2026-07-22
- Official constraint: Facebook groups define their own rules; posting or participation can require approval or be restricted.
- Sources: [group rules](https://www.facebook.com/help/462230500886400/), [group participation restrictions](https://www.facebook.com/help/158630788063039)
- Volatility rule: inspect the target page or group's current rules before drafting.

## Purpose and audience behavior

Use Facebook for accessible explanation, page updates, customer-oriented discussion, events, stories, and value-led participation in relevant groups. Page followers may know the brand; group members share an interest, not an obligation to welcome promotion.

## Formats

Support standard page posts, community-group posts, launches, educational posts, events, customer stories, short versions, and discussion-oriented versions.

## Hook and body

- For pages, begin with the change, lesson, use case, event, or customer problem.
- For groups, begin with a useful question, observation, process, or resource that fits an existing discussion.
- Explain technical language and use a narrative pace suitable for mixed awareness levels.
- Keep one main action.

## Group package

Generate a value-first version, transparent disclosure, optional non-promotional discussion version, link-placement recommendation, and moderation-risk note. Record the rule URL, date checked, direct-promotion posture, post-approval requirements, and relevant disclosure rules. If rules are unclear, recommend asking moderators or not posting.

## CTA, links, and visuals

Use a direct page CTA when appropriate. In groups, match CTA strength to the rules; a question or no-link contribution may be safer. Do not hide affiliation. Provide useful alt text and captions; keep key event details in text, not only an image.

## Failure modes

Reject page copy pasted into a group, generic “thoughts?” engagement bait, undisclosed customer stories, fictional quotations, overlong setup, and claims that a link placement or image format guarantees distribution.

## Validation checklist

- Distinguish page from group context.
- Record current group rules and moderator risk.
- Include disclosure where relevant.
- Verify event details, offer terms, links, and proof.
- Supply accessibility text.
