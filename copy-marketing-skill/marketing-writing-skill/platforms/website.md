# Website

## Purpose and structure

Use for a homepage or general marketing site that must orient multiple visitor types while preserving a clear primary path.

1. Hero: audience-relevant value, clarifying subhead, primary CTA.
2. Recognition: problem or situation visitors identify.
3. Value: outcomes tied to mechanisms.
4. Fit or use cases: help visitors self-select.
5. Proof: verified evidence near claims.
6. Process or product explanation.
7. Objections and risk reduction.
8. Final CTA.

## Execution

- **Opening:** Say what the offer helps whom accomplish. A slogan may support, not replace, comprehension.
- **Length:** Match page complexity; keep the hero concise and let sections answer one question each.
- **Density:** Headings must carry the scan. Keep paragraphs short; use cards only for parallel concepts.
- **CTA:** One primary action, with a lower-commitment secondary action when readiness varies.
- **Formatting:** Use semantic heading order, descriptive links, accessible labels, and meaningful button text.
- **Hashtags:** None.
- **SEO:** Assign one primary search intent per page. Use natural topic language in title, H1, opening, headings, body, links, and image alt text where relevant.
- **Metadata:** Pair with [metadata](metadata.md); avoid duplicating the same title or description across pages.

## Prohibited patterns

No hero made entirely of wordplay, three unrelated primary CTAs, unsupported logo walls, vague feature grids, autoplay assumptions, or repeated claims without new support.

## Edit

Run a five-second clarity test: what is it, who is it for, why care, and what next? Then test the heading-only scan and mobile order.
