## Adapter contract

Every new adapter must specify: purpose, recommended structure, opening style, working length, paragraph density, CTA style, formatting constraints, hashtag use, hard or practical character limits, SEO requirements, metadata requirements, prohibited patterns, and editing rules.

Platform limits and policies can change. Treat numeric limits as working guidance unless the user supplies current specifications or requests verification. For paid distribution or publication-critical limits, verify against the platform's current official documentation.

All adapters inherit the [central-idea invariant](../workflow/central-idea.md): change expression, not the factual promise.
