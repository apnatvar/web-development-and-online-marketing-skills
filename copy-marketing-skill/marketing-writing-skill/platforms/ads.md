# Ads

## Purpose and structure

Use for paid search, paid social, display, and short promotional placements. Identify audience, placement, objective, traffic temperature, and destination before drafting.

1. Hook or query match.
2. Specific value or relevant problem.
3. Differentiator or proof, space permitting.
4. Clear action aligned with the landing page.

## Execution

- **Opening:** For search, match intent and decision language. For social, interrupt through relevance or concrete contrast, not shock.
- **Length:** Draft to the exact placement fields when supplied. Otherwise produce modular short, medium, and long components; verify current official limits before trafficking.
- **Density:** One idea per ad. Creative and copy must divide the communication deliberately.
- **CTA:** Match the campaign goal and landing-page action.
- **Formatting:** Supply variants by strategic angle, not synonym. Label headline, primary text, description, and CTA.
- **Hashtags:** Usually none for paid placements unless a campaign or community convention requires them.
- **SEO:** Paid search needs keyword-intent match, not organic keyword density.
- **Metadata:** Record audience, placement, angle, offer, proof, destination, and experiment variable.

## Prohibited patterns

No unsupported outcomes, personal-attribute insinuations, misleading buttons, false scarcity, bait-and-switch, fake social proof, or policy evasion. Platform advertising policies change; verify official requirements before launch.

## Edit

Read the ad and landing-page hero as one sentence. Confirm the creative does not imply more than the copy, and vary only one testing dimension per controlled experiment.
