# Email

## Purpose and structure

Use for outbound, nurture, launch, lifecycle, sales follow-up, newsletters, and retention. First identify the email type, relationship, permission context, and one desired action.

1. Subject: accurate reason to open.
2. Preview text: extend rather than repeat the subject.
3. Opening: relevant context or reader situation.
4. Body: one claim, value, or update with support.
5. CTA: one clear next step.
6. Close: appropriate human or brand sign-off.

## Execution

- **Opening:** Earn attention through relevance, not false familiarity.
- **Length:** About 50–200 words for focused sales or lifecycle email; newsletters may be longer when subscribers expect depth.
- **Density:** Short paragraphs; make the action understandable without image loading.
- **CTA:** One primary action. Use a direct reply when dialogue is the desired step.
- **Formatting:** Mobile-first, descriptive links, restrained bolding, compliant sender identity and unsubscribe treatment where required.
- **Hashtags:** None.
- **Character guidance:** Keep subjects concise—often under roughly 50 characters when possible—and put critical preview meaning early. Verify client-specific constraints when required.
- **SEO:** Not relevant; optimize for recognition, deliverability, comprehension, and trust.
- **Metadata:** Provide subject and preview text with each draft unless excluded.

## Prohibited patterns

No deceptive “Re:” prefixes, fake personalization, false urgency, undisclosed conditions, manipulative guilt, too many links, or claims unsupported by the destination page.

## Edit

Compare subject, preview, body promise, and destination. Test plain text, mobile scanning, and whether a recipient can decline without friction.
