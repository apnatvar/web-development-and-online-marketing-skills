# Blog

## Purpose and structure

Use for education, search discovery, perspective, category creation, evidence-led argument, and durable founder expertise.

1. Title that accurately promises the article's value.
2. Opening that states relevance and main answer early.
3. Compact roadmap when the piece is complex.
4. Hierarchical sections that answer reader questions.
5. Examples, evidence, and counterpoints.
6. Synthesis and a next step proportionate to intent.

## Execution

- **Opening:** Avoid long anecdotes or trend claims before revealing the subject.
- **Length:** Let search intent and complexity determine depth; do not target word count for its own sake.
- **Density:** Short paragraphs, descriptive H2/H3 headings, selective lists, and tables for exact mappings.
- **CTA:** Continue learning, use a template, compare an approach, subscribe, or evaluate the offer based on intent.
- **Formatting:** Use one H1, logical heading order, descriptive links, and accessible media.
- **Hashtags:** None on the article itself.
- **SEO:** Satisfy the query completely, distinguish primary and secondary intent, use natural terminology, and link to relevant internal pages.
- **Metadata:** Provide title, meta description, slug suggestion, canonical recommendation if duplication is plausible, and social preview when requested.

## Prohibited patterns

No padded introductions, unsupported statistics, stitched-together keyword sections, fake expertise, copied definitions, repetitive conclusions, or a sales pitch that interrupts informational intent.

## Edit

Check the heading-only argument, source every external factual claim, remove sections that answer a different query, and ensure the CTA follows naturally from the reader's job.
