# Search and social metadata

## Purpose

Represent the page accurately in search results and link previews while supporting the page's primary intent.

## Fields

- **Title:** Lead with the page's distinctive topic or offer; add brand only when useful.
- **Meta description:** Summarize value and fit, adding a credible reason to visit.
- **Slug:** Short, readable, stable, and descriptive.
- **Canonical:** Point to the preferred URL when equivalent pages exist; do not invent a canonicalization policy.
- **Social title/description:** Adapt for shared context without changing the claim.
- **Alt text:** Describe the image's relevant content and function; do not use it as an SEO slogan.

## Working guidance

Search engines and social platforms may rewrite or truncate fields. Treat roughly 50–60 characters for titles and 140–160 for descriptions as drafting heuristics, not guaranteed display limits. Optimize important meaning early and verify current requirements when exact rendering matters.

## SEO rules

Match one page intent, use natural language, keep every page's metadata distinct, and align title, H1, body, URL, and promise. Metadata cannot rescue a page that fails the query.

## Prohibited patterns

No keyword lists, clickbait unsupported by the page, identical sitewide descriptions, fabricated prices or ratings, unnecessary all caps, or alt text stuffed with search terms.

## Edit

Count characters for delivery convenience, then judge clarity rather than forcing arbitrary limits. Compare the snippet with likely neighboring results and the on-page hero.
