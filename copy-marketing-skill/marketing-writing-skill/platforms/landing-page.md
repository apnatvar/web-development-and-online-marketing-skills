# Landing page

## Purpose and structure

Use for one audience, one campaign or offer, and one primary conversion.

1. Message-matched hero: promise, audience/context, CTA.
2. Stakes: supported problem and desired change.
3. Mechanism: how the offer works.
4. Benefits: prioritized, concrete outcomes.
5. Proof: demonstration, data, customers, or reasoning.
6. Alternatives and differentiation.
7. Objections, risk, and fit.
8. Repeated CTA with clarified expectation.

## Execution

- **Opening:** Match the ad, email, search query, or referral promise that brought the visitor.
- **Length:** Use the shortest page that resolves the decision. Higher novelty, risk, or price generally needs more explanation and proof.
- **Density:** One decision question per section; descriptive headings and compact paragraphs.
- **CTA:** One primary conversion, repeated at logical decision points. State what happens after the click.
- **Formatting:** Remove navigation when campaign focus requires it, but retain trust and accessibility essentials.
- **Hashtags:** None.
- **SEO:** Apply only when the page is intended for organic discovery; campaign pages may prioritize message match.
- **Metadata:** Create a distinct title and description; use social-preview copy when sharing matters.

## Prohibited patterns

No fake countdowns, invented scarcity, unverified testimonials, hidden conditions, mismatched ad promises, vague “Get started” buttons without context, or benefit lists detached from mechanisms.

## Edit

Trace each claim to proof, test the hero against the traffic source, remove secondary exits that conflict with the objective, and inspect the page as a skeptical mobile visitor.
