# Instagram

## Purpose and structure

Use for visual-led education, identity, community, concise story, and creator or founder perspective. The caption should complement the asset rather than narrate what viewers can already see.

1. Open with a concrete promise, tension, or scene.
2. Give context that the image or video cannot.
3. Deliver one idea in skimmable blocks.
4. Close with one relevant action.

## Execution

- **Opening:** Front-load meaning before caption truncation. For carousels, coordinate the caption hook with slide one without duplicating it.
- **Length:** Prefer 150–800 characters for simple assets and up to about 1,500 when the caption adds real depth. Confirm current hard limits when critical.
- **Density:** One to three short sentences per paragraph.
- **CTA:** Save, share, comment with a specific experience, visit the link destination, or take the next content step.
- **Formatting:** Use whitespace, restrained emoji, and numbered steps only where they improve scanning.
- **Hashtags:** Use a small, relevant set tied to topic, audience, or category; do not add a generic block for appearance.
- **SEO:** Put plain-language topic phrases in the caption and align alt text with the actual visual. Do not keyword-stuff.
- **Metadata:** Supply descriptive alt text when requested; distinguish accessibility description from promotional copy.

## Prohibited patterns

No irrelevant trending tags, engagement bait, inaccessible emoji walls, copied LinkedIn formatting, unsupported transformation claims, or captions that ignore the visual.

## Edit

Check the opening without the image, then the caption with the image. Remove duplicated visual information, verify the CTA destination, and ensure the content works without hashtag reach.
