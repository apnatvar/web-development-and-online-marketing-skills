# Before-and-after example

## Input

> RelayNote is an innovative all-in-one platform that revolutionizes research collaboration. Our seamless solution saves time, boosts productivity, and empowers teams to make better decisions. Get started today!

## Diagnosis

- **Audience:** missing.
- **Problem:** generic “collaboration” rather than the supported record-to-decision gap.
- **Positioning:** “all-in-one platform” creates breadth without a useful frame.
- **Differentiation:** adjectives replace mechanism.
- **Proof:** time, productivity, and decision-quality outcomes are unsupported.
- **Memorability:** no concrete scene or contrast.
- **CTA:** too large for an unproven and unfamiliar offer.

## Revised version

### Keep each research decision connected to its source records

RelayNote lets small research teams link a decision note to the records they discussed. Instead of reconstructing the reasoning from a summary document and scattered chat, reviewers can see the source context beside the conclusion.

**View the example workflow**

## Why it is stronger

The revision names a best-fit audience, makes the problem observable, explains the mechanism, contrasts a realistic workflow, reduces claim strength to the available evidence, and uses a lower-risk CTA.

## Shortened version

Link each research decision to the source records behind it. RelayNote keeps the conclusion and its context in one reviewable workflow.

## Expansion path

Do not repeat the promise. Add a product demonstration, verified customer example, supported compatibility detail, or clear limitation. Until evidence exists, label outcome claims as hypotheses.
