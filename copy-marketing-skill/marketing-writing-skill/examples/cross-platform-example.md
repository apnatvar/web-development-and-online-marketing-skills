# Cross-platform example

## Neutral scenario

“RelayNote” is a fictional workspace that lets small research teams attach decisions to source records. Known facts: records and decisions can be linked; no performance data or customer testimony is available.

## Central-idea card

- **Audience:** research leads at small teams using documents and chat
- **Tension:** the final decision becomes separated from the evidence behind it
- **Claim:** RelayNote gives each research decision a visible path back to its source records
- **Mechanism:** users link a decision note to the records discussed
- **Contrast:** unlike a standalone summary document, the decision stays connected to source context
- **Proof:** capability demonstration only; outcome proof is needed
- **Action:** view the example workflow
- **Boundary:** do not claim fewer errors, faster decisions, or universal traceability

## LinkedIn

The hard part of a research decision is not always making it.

It is reconstructing why the team made it three weeks later.

A summary records the answer. It often loses the path: which source changed the team's view, what tradeoff was accepted, and where the caveat lives.

RelayNote links a decision note to the source records discussed. The point is simple: keep the conclusion close to its context.
