# Editing

Edit from strategy to surface.

## Pass 1: argument

- Does every section support the central idea?
- Is the claim relevant, differentiated, and properly qualified?
- Does evidence support the nearest claim?
- Is the CTA proportionate to awareness and trust?

## Pass 2: specificity

- Replace vague nouns and verbs with observable detail.
- Distinguish feature, mechanism, benefit, and outcome.
- Remove unsupported superlatives, universals, and certainty.
- Convert abstract examples into truthful scenes or clearly hypothetical examples.

## Pass 3: compression

- Remove duplicate setup, throat-clearing, summaries, filler transitions, and adjective chains.
- Preserve claim, causal logic, critical proof, meaningful contrast, and CTA.
- For expansion, add depth by answering a reader question—not by restating.

## Pass 4: voice and rhythm

Vary sentence length naturally. Prefer strong verbs. Remove canned AI patterns: “in today's fast-paced world,” “unlock,” “revolutionize,” “delve,” “game-changer,” empty rhetorical questions, forced three-part lists, excessive em dashes, and generic conclusions.

## Pass 5: channel compliance

Recheck length, formatting, links, hashtags, metadata, SEO intent, CTA, and any paid-platform policy constraints.
