# Customer-centred narrative

Use when the company dominates the story, the journey is unclear, or the CTA appears before the audience understands the stakes.

## Operating principles

- Make the customer the actor; make the brand a capable guide.
- Define what the customer wants in concrete terms.
- Show the external obstacle, the practical or emotional consequence, and the broader reason it matters only when evidence supports them.
- Establish understanding and competence without self-congratulation.
- Give a short, plausible plan that reduces uncertainty.
- Use direct and transitional calls to action according to readiness.
- Show success and the cost of inaction proportionately; avoid melodrama.

## Narrative spine

“You want `[outcome]`, but `[obstacle]` creates `[consequence]`. We help through `[credible mechanism/plan]`, so you can `[specific success]`. Start with `[next step]`.”

Use the spine to organize, then remove any formulaic feel in the final prose.

## Misuse guards

Do not force every asset into a complete story. Do not exaggerate failure. Do not erase the product's actual mechanism in favor of emotional language.
