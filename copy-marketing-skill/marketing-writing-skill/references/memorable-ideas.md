# Memorable ideas

Use when the message is accurate but abstract, bloated, unsurprising, or difficult to retell.

## Construction principles

- **Core:** Remove competing messages until one useful idea remains.
- **Attention:** Create a truthful gap between expectation and reality.
- **Concrete:** Replace abstractions with observable actions, objects, moments, and examples.
- **Credible:** Use appropriate proof, detail, demonstration, or transparent reasoning.
- **Emotional:** Connect the outcome to a human stake the audience actually values.
- **Story:** Show cause, choice, friction, and change instead of decorating claims with anecdotes.

## Practical moves

Turn “streamline collaboration” into a concrete scene such as “reviewers comment on the same approved version instead of three email attachments.” Use a compact contrast, analogy, demonstration, or counterexample the audience can repeat.

## Misuse guards

Do not use surprise that distorts the truth. Do not invent vivid customer scenes and present them as real. Do not let a story obscure the claim or CTA.
