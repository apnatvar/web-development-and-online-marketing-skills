# Principle library

Load only the files that match the diagnosed constraint.

| Constraint | Reference | Framework-level source |
|---|---|---|
| Unreliable customer assumptions | [Customer discovery](customer-discovery.md) | *The Mom Test*, Rob Fitzpatrick |
| Objections, tension, or high-stakes dialogue | [Negotiation](negotiation.md) | *Never Split the Difference*, Chris Voss with Tahl Raz |
| Confused category or competitive frame | [Positioning](positioning.md) | *Positioning*, Al Ries and Jack Trout |
| Weak relevance or unclear best-fit customer | [Differentiation](differentiation.md) | *Obviously Awesome*, April Dunford |
| Forgettable or abstract message | [Memorable ideas](memorable-ideas.md) | *Made to Stick*, Chip Heath and Dan Heath |
| Brand-centred or confusing journey | [Narrative](narrative.md) | *Building a StoryBrand*, Donald Miller |
| Buried conclusion or disordered logic | [Structured communication](structured-communication.md) | *The Pyramid Principle*, Barbara Minto |
| Premature pitching or shallow sales message | [Diagnostic selling](diagnostic-selling.md) | *SPIN Selling*, Neil Rackham |
| Message mismatched to market sophistication | [Market awareness](market-awareness.md) | *Breakthrough Advertising*, Eugene M. Schwartz |
| Scattered objectives or slogan-like strategy | [Strategy](strategy.md) | *Good Strategy Bad Strategy*, Richard Rumelt |

These files paraphrase and operationalize ideas; they do not substitute for or reproduce the books.
