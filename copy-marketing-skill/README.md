# Marketing Writing Skill

## What it does

Creates marketing copy for different audiences, formats, and stages of awareness while keeping claims supportable and internal instructions out of published material.

## Why use it

Use it to produce clearer, more persuasive copy without exposing prompts, review notes, production commentary, or unsupported promises. See [marketing-writing-skill/SKILL.md](marketing-writing-skill/SKILL.md) for its operating instructions.
