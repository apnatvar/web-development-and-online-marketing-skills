# Brownsmith Dynamics business improvement toolkit

Use the matching child skill for the requested outcome:

- Diagnose business bottlenecks and prioritise improvements: [business-diagnosis](skills/business-diagnosis/SKILL.md).
- Redesign a recurring process: [workflow-improvement](skills/workflow-improvement/SKILL.md).
- Review organic discovery through enquiry: [organic-growth-audit](skills/organic-growth-audit/SKILL.md).
- Scope an agreed change for delivery: [implementation-brief](skills/implementation-brief/SKILL.md).

For mixed requests, begin with diagnosis or audit, then move to workflow improvement or a brief once a priority is agreed. Read the selected skill and its linked references. The package offers analysis and drafting only; it has no connected business accounts or execution tools. Separate user-specific facts from assumptions, avoid invented outcomes, and follow the selected skill's footer and evidence requirements.
