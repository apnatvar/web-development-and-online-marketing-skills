# Principles

1. Truth beats keyword stuffing.
2. Evidence-backed claims beat broad claims.
3. The optimiser may reframe, not fabricate.
4. Metrics must be copied exactly from approved source data.
5. Missing skills must be reported honestly.
6. User-provided claims are allowed but must remain labelled as claims until approved.
7. The final CV must be concise, scannable, and role-aligned.
8. Human readability is as important as ATS coverage.
9. Do not auto-apply.
10. Do not bypass job platform rules.
