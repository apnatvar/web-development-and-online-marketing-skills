# Quality and Accessibility

## ATS and document structure

- Use standard headings and a simple reading order.
- Prefer text over icons, text boxes, charts, and image-only content.
- Keep dates, employers, job titles, and locations consistent.
- Expand uncommon abbreviations once when space permits.
- Use job-description terminology only when evidence supports it.
- Do not claim an ATS score as objective or guaranteed; explain the coverage method instead.

## Editorial review

- Preserve the candidate's level of formality and regional spelling unless asked to change it.
- Remove repetition, vague adjectives, and unsupported superlatives.
- Ensure each bullet is understandable without proprietary context.
- Check that edits have not changed ownership, team size, seniority, causality, or metrics.
- Separate substantive gaps from optional improvements.

## Privacy and fairness

- Minimise exposure of addresses, identification numbers, birth dates, family status, and other sensitive data.
- Do not infer protected or sensitive traits from names, dates, locations, photographs, or writing style.
- Do not advise adding or removing personal characteristics to facilitate discrimination.
- If the user requests a market convention involving personal data, explain the tradeoff and let the user decide.

## Final handoff

Provide the requested artifact, a short list of material changes, all confirmation items, and a manual submission checklist. Keep rejected or unsupported claims out of the final artifact.
