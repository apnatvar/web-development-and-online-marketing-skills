# Crawlability and indexability guidance

Load only for technical or combined audits.

Check:

- final HTTP status and redirect chain;
- authentication and crawler access;
- robots.txt rules and sitemap discovery;
- robots meta/X-Robots-Tag;
- canonical consistency;
- internal links and orphan risks;
- rendered JavaScript content and links;
- error/soft-error behavior;
- sitemap URLs versus canonical/indexable URLs.

Do not claim that a page is indexed from local or public HTML alone. Search Console or search-engine evidence is required. Do not treat robots.txt as a privacy or deindexing mechanism.
